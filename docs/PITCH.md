# Investor pitch — plan

Framework: Matt Heusch, “How your presentation to investors should be”. Presenter: **Beatriz**.
Deck draft: the “loop. — Investor Pitch” Slides artifact (link in the team chat).

## Rules from the brief
- Prepare for **80–90 % of the time**: for a 5-minute slot, aim for **4:15–4:30**.
- Demo-day deck: **max ~10 words per slide**, big fonts, animated.
- Rehearse **30 times** (yes, really).
- Sell the **problem** before the solution; show how we’re **different, not just better**; show **velocity**.
